## Conclusion - keep the current defaults

Anne's classes run in a community centre with a gentle, accessible, community-oriented format - not a heated boutique studio. The brief states the below-studio pricing is intentional. The data supports the current defaults:

- **€12 drop-in** undercuts commercial studios (€18–22) by 35–45% while staying above the very cheapest community listings - consistent with "accessible but not devalued".
- The **multi-date €11 rate** mirrors the community-class pattern of rewarding block booking (€11 vs €18 in the Facebook example).
- **€50/€95 passes** (€10/€9.50 per class) are honest savings vs drop-in and sit well below studio pass equivalents.
- **€75 monthly** matches the low end of the Dublin unlimited market (~€80) - sensible for a 2-class-per-week schedule (max 8–9 classes/month ≈ €8.60/class).
- **€720 annual** ≈ 2 months free vs monthly - a standard structure.

**No changes made.** All of these remain fully editable by Anne in **Admin → Pricing**, and the pricing simulator shows the effect of any change before it goes live.
